## Design comparison and remaining implementation gates

The comparison standard uses documented features of earth.nullschool.net, NASA Eyes and Global Fishing Watch: visible source/date/units, meaningful mark inspection, honest scale and uncertainty, documented filtering, accessible pause controls and reproducible displayed subsets. No fabricated 8.5+/10 competitor scores or claim of outperforming their tested products is made.

All accepted designs distinguish measured marks from decorative motion. Nine reusable motion recipes support 73 concept-specific contracts; they do not imply 73 independently coded animation systems. Decorative loops cannot change values, encode nonexistent flows, invent forecasts or conceal caveats. Contracts require immediate pause on interaction, explicit restart, reduced-motion equivalents, keyboard/table access, mobile selection targets and bounded scene costs. Their geometry, scales, filtering and backside discovery were reviewed as specifications.

**Product quality remains unrated.** Implementation must still pass actual browser and representative-device checks for rendering correctness, keyboard and screen-reader behavior, mobile touch usability, reduced motion, frame time, memory, context recovery, route transitions and task-based engagement. The user’s seven product criteria—including coding and scalability—cannot be certified from this research pack or from design prose. Source freshness must be rechecked before public implementation, especially changing policy pages, company announcements and current database snapshots.

Reuse approval is limited to the packaged facts and original explanations under each recorded basis. A factual extract from a restricted article does not license its prose, charts or imagery. Native benchmark scores are not atlas-wide danger or impact scores. No new forecast is supplied. Direct AI connection does not automatically mean a causal AI effect; 27 accepted concepts are explicitly contextual.
