# AFTERWORLDS — evidence and motion design pack

This pack contains a specific creative treatment for all 132 research concepts and field-linked designs for 73 independently accepted research specifications. The other 59 remain held research drafts. The registry adopts acceptance only when the final reviewer verdict, all ten gates, score of at least 92/100, design identity and exact current dataset hashes agree. This is specification readiness, not a product-quality score.

The 73 accepted specifications use 73 distinct geometric compositions: five geographic studies, nine global-statistic objects and 59 explicitly abstract data globes. Most available evidence concerns categories, trials, policies or global quantities. Their spheres are reading instruments, not maps. No random location, fabricated backside observation or artificial future state is supplied to make a globe look busier.

## The motion grammar

A living globe does not require invented data. Each matched composition has a slow, intentional loop on its own instrument frame: a turning gimbal, a tracing seam, a gliding measurement bracket, a shallow page tilt, a precessing cage or a closed decorative return line. Periods range from 36 to 74 seconds. Every quantity, colour scale, interval, category, source date and geographic position stays fixed throughout the loop.

The motion label is **Ambient frame motion — not measured change**. Pause is always visible. Interaction pauses the loop until the reader explicitly plays it again. Reduced motion opens with the complete static evidence frame; the table and direct rotation remain usable. There is no automatic growth, flashing, fake traffic, moving vessel, migrating bird or clinical heartbeat.

Each loop adds at most two draw calls and 2,000 triangles. The active visible scene alone runs; offscreen, hidden or paused scenes stop. These are implementation budgets, not measured performance claims. If a representative phone misses its budget, disable ambient motion before removing any observations.

## Implementation and release boundary

The final independent audit accepts 73 specifications for implementation. Bind each scene to its exact `datasetBindings`; a later source change requires re-review. Verify the source digest, declared fields, included row IDs and transform. The globe and accessible table use the same selected rows; explicit unknown values remain unknown. Contextual findings keep their contextual label. Source-native scores retain their definitions and never become an editorial 1–100 impact rating.

Five designs have true geographic encodings: electricity access, inventor-location counts, named programme partners, population/connectivity and the Norwegian recorder network. Country summaries use source geography with audited polygon joins. The recorder study uses source-published, deliberately randomised coordinates with the approximation disclosed. Abstract designs have no coastlines or latitude labels.

On touch devices, 44px selection proxies do not inflate measured marks. The visible Move/Read toggle gives scrolling priority when reading; Next observation and the semantic table reach every row without drag or hover. At 320px the selection card and controls form one column. The table exposes row ID, value, unit, date, qualifier, status and source locator, plus each design's bound fields.

Before release, test scale endpoints, null/bound/zero distinctions, exact filters, unchanged quantities at four motion phases, screen-reader parity, keyboard/touch navigation, reduced motion and 20 route changes without retained contexts/listeners. Measure frame rate on named mobile and desktop devices. A research-readiness result does not waive animation, usability, engagement, accessibility, coding or scalability testing.

## Competitive standard

The independent benchmark document in `research/audits/DESIGN_BENCHMARKS.md` uses earth.nullschool, NASA Eyes, Global Fishing Watch and World Processor as reference standards. This pack adopts their emphasis on visible units, provenance, meaningful selection, honest temporal controls and explained modelling. AFTERWORLDS aims to add concise editorial questions and a wider range of visual arguments. That is a design ambition, not a claim that these products or an unimplemented scene have measured 8.5/10 or 9.2/10 quality scores.
